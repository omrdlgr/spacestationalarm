Space Station Alarm

Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 July 2026

The short version: the app collects nothing, sends us nothing, and has no account. Your location stays on your device.

What the app uses your location for

A satellite pass depends on where you are standing. With your permission, the app reads your location on your device to work out which passes are visible from where you are and when to alert you. That location is never transmitted to us, never stored on a server, and never shared with anyone. It stays on the device.

What the app sends over the network

To predict passes, the app downloads publicly available orbital data for the space stations from CelesTrak. This is a plain request for public data. It does not include your location, your identity, or anything about you.

Data we collect

None. There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no account, and no third-party SDK that gathers data. We do not know who you are or that you use the app.

Notifications

Alarms are local notifications scheduled on your own device and Apple Watch. They are not sent through any server of ours.

Children

The app is safe for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.
